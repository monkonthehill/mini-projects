✦ The Hands Behind The Magic ✦

About The Artist

"Every stitch is a spell. Every character is a small soul."

I'm Yukta — a self-taught crochet artist, fantasy nerd, and collector of dark fairy tales. What started as a lockdown hobby became an obsession, and then a calling. Baba Yagga Store was born from my love of anime, gaming, folklore, and the urge to hold the things I love in my hands.

I specialize in amigurumi — the Japanese art of crocheting small stuffed characters — with a dark, maximalist twist. Skulls with bows. Witches with attitude. Dragons that glow in the dark. Each piece takes between 4 to 30 hours to complete.

I work from a small studio in Shimla, surrounded by yarn, incense, and a frankly irresponsible number of plushies.
